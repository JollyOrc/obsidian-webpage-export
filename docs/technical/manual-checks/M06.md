## Steps

1. Start Obsidian with this vault.
2. Settings → Community plugins: the plugin shows **`1.9.2-roa.1-diag.1`**.
   If not, stop. Do not accept an offered update.
3. Export as usual. **Keep the export folder `~/Desktop/RoA Export`; do not
   choose `~/Downloads/roa-export-diag`.**
4. If Obsidian freezes or goes white, wait 2 minutes, then quit. If the
   export ends, wait 30 seconds.
5. Tell Claude what happened and roughly when. The log is the newest
   `~/Downloads/roa-export-diag/diag-*.tsv`.
