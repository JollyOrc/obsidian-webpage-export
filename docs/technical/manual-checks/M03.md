## Result (2026-10-30, run 1)

Log: `~/Downloads/roa-export-diag/diag-2026-10-30T11-40-08-509Z.tsv`
(1 725 lines). The user reports: froze on the magic skills note at about
12:41 local (11:41 UTC); the window went white and did not respond; the app
menu still worked and Obsidian was quit normally.

Measured:

- 231 files started, 230 `ok`; every one of them passed `sections-counted`
  within its 4 000 ms limit.
- `1.4.3 Magic Skills.md` (started 48 551 ms): `sections-counted` timed out
  (4 121 ms, 10 of 15 sections, as in M02); the fallback renderer ran
  sections 1 to 4, then section 5 entered `renderer.measureSection` at
  54 353 ms and the log ends there.
- Heartbeats every second up to 53 010 ms; the one due at about 54 010 ms
  was not written, nor any later line: the main thread stopped (a
  synchronous freeze, not an endless asynchronous wait).
- Memory: heap 298 MB at the start, 550 MB after the first file, then
  550 to 580 MB to the end; DOM nodes 1 600 to 4 100. No steady growth.

Across M01 to M03: every freeze followed the 4 s `sections-counted` timeout
on this note and happened inside the fallback renderer, at a different step
each time (M01 after the loop, M03 in `measureSection` of section 5); alone
(M02) the same fallback finished.

Not established (SCAR-024): the mechanism. Unverified explanation: the main
renderer is still drawing the remaining sections (M02: all 15 at 7.1 s) when
the fallback re-renders and measures the same `renderer.sections` objects,
and the two interfere, so the outcome depends on timing.
