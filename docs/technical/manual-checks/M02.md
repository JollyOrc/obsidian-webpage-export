## Result (2026-10-30, run 1)

Log: `~/Downloads/roa-export-diag/diag-2026-10-30T11-24-58-575Z.tsv`
(211 lines). The user reports: no freeze, the export finished.

Measured:

- The note alone exports `ok` in 8 070 ms; batch 9 002 ms.
- Main renderer: `sections-counted` timed out at 4 000 ms with 10 of 15
  sections in the sizer; the condition became true at 7 133 ms
  (`late-success`). The fallback renderer then ran all 15 sections in
  1 260 ms (`fb-loop-end`), `Promise.all` over 62 post-process promises took
  753 ms.
- All 8 `text-mapper` blocks in the embedded spell notes were filled when
  first seen, each one `<svg>` with 297 to 319 shapes, 45 to 54 kB.
- M01 reread: its log also shows 15 fallback sections (of 15), so in M01 the
  fallback loop finished and the freeze came after it, inside
  `renderMarkdownView`, before `file ok`: `Promise.all(promises)`, the
  section clone, or title and icon. diag.2 has no lines there.

Not established (SCAR-024): why the same note froze after 226 other files
